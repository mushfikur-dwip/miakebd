PASTE THIS INTO:  dev/resources/views/app.blade.php

Put it inside the <head> section, just before the closing </head> tag,
AFTER @inertiaHead.

------------------------------ COPY FROM HERE ------------------------------

        {{-- Structured data (JSON-LD) shared from the controller.
             Rendered server-side because Vue templates cannot contain <script>. --}}
        @isset($structuredData)
            <script type="application/ld+json">{!! $structuredData !!}</script>
        @endisset

------------------------------- TO HERE ------------------------------------


YOUR app.blade.php SHOULD END UP LOOKING ROUGHLY LIKE THIS:

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">

    @routes
    @vite(['resources/js/app.js'])
    @inertiaHead

    {{-- paste the block here --}}
    @isset($structuredData)
        <script type="application/ld+json">{!! $structuredData !!}</script>
    @endisset
</head>
<body class="font-sans antialiased">
    @inertia
</body>
</html>

  curl -s https://suglow.com/product/YOUR-PRODUCT-SLUG | grep -A3 "ld+json"

  https://search.google.com/test/rich-results
